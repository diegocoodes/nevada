# Direção de arte Retém

As referências fornecidas orientam composição e hierarquia, com conteúdo e identidade Retém. O hero atual da estética usa fundo preto com luz vermelha difusa, tipografia vermelha ampliada e um carro vermelho em perfil. A direção e o prompt atuais estão em [retem-profile-hero.md](retem-profile-hero.md). As imagens abaixo documentam as versões anteriores, preservadas no projeto. A landing da distribuidora usa fotografia de material, luz vermelha, chamadas centralizadas e seções de produtos e etapas. Fora dos heros, as páginas compartilham a atmosfera de carvão e vermelho; o catálogo usa amarelo nos destaques. Manrope compõe os títulos e Inter os textos. A abertura integra o efeito de texto em partículas fornecido pelo usuário.

Imagens ilustrativas geradas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, sem representar um veículo atendido pela Retém ou um produto específico de Nevada Films ou Origin Películas. As imagens foram codificadas em WebP para uso no site; os arquivos anteriores foram preservados.

## Prompt do hero

Use case: ads-marketing. Create an ultra-wide cinematic automotive website hero background, 1792x1024 landscape. Photorealistic luxury red sports coupe seen from directly above, front pointing left, body occupying the right 60 percent of frame, rear clipped by the right edge, beautiful accurate vehicle proportions, glass roof and sharp white specular reflections, deep glossy scarlet car paint. Rich red studio floor with dramatic soft vermilion light from upper right and deep wine red shadows. Left 45 percent must remain dark and uncluttered as negative space for HTML headline. Fade lower edge into near black. High-end automotive art direction, editorial brochure photography, fine subtle analog texture. No words, no lettering, no logos, no badge, no watermark. This is a background image for Retém automotive care, inspired by a red and black overhead sports-car art-directed composition.

## Prompt da distribuidora

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium automotive film distributor landing page atmospheric hero photography. 1536x1024 landscape. On a pure black studio background, a sculptural arrangement of two unbranded rolls of transparent automotive protection film and a single sweeping curved sheet of smoked window tint. One central upright clear film roll and one shorter horizontal roll at the base, no packaging, no labels. Strong dramatic red and scarlet neon rim lighting from behind, clean white specular edge highlights across transparent film and cylindrical core. A large graceful clear sheet rises in an arc like a flowing ribbon with subtle red reflections. Product composition centered in the upper two-thirds, black negative space bottom quarter for HTML typography. Glossy black floor subtle reflections. Sophisticated restrained luxury product CGI and commercial photography, physically plausible transparent materials, crisp sharp product details, deep shadows, luminous red accents. No people, no robots, no text, no logos, no watermark. Cinematic, striking, a visual about automotive film protection and professional application.
